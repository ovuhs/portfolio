Privacy

This is a personal portfolio. It counts visits so I can see which work people look at. I do not sell or share this data.

What is recorded when you visit

The pages you open and what you click (for example a project, the CV download or a contact link), your device type, browser, operating system, screen size, language and time zone, and the site you came from. It does not include your name, email address or IP address.

A random ID is kept in your browser's local storage so repeat visits can be told apart. I set no cookies of my own.

I also use Google Analytics (with IP anonymisation) for overall traffic numbers. Google sets its own cookies for that.

Vercel Web Analytics, which sets no cookies, counts page views as well.

The contact form

If you send a message, I keep your name, email address and message so I can reply. Ask me and I will delete it.
